# Five-minute community demo

This demo shows how Codex can connect product research, creative production, and measurable learning without hiding the deterministic parts behind a model.

## Audience

Developers, ecommerce founders, growth teams, and first-time AI builders.

## Preparation

1. Start Winning Product Finder in demo mode.
2. Search for `led`, inspect Hook Radar, and copy the JSON response or a UGC brief.
3. Start AdForge with the default local engine.
4. Use a sample product image and paste the research output into competitor research.

## Live flow

1. Preview the detected psychological angles.
2. Generate several evidence-backed hooks without copying source text.
3. Render one free local video with synchronized captions and an AI disclosure.
4. Compare predicted scores and explain which parts are deterministic.
5. Enter sample campaign results and show how real performance changes future angle weights.

## Learning outcome

Participants leave with a reusable pattern: collect evidence, separate rules from model judgment, generate alternatives, measure outcomes, and feed verified results back into the next iteration.

## Workshop extension

In a 60-minute workshop, teams can bring one product, produce three hooks, render one variant, and present the evidence behind their creative decision. Publish anonymized outputs and lessons as a community recap.
